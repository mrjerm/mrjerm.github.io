Rime
Daily ETF regime allocator · Python, pandas, Alpaca

A daily allocator that splits capital between leveraged Nasdaq ETF exposure, a volatility hedge, and cash, using a shallow regime tree with a macro overlay and a volatility circuit breaker. An intraday gold system running long and short as two separate risk sleeves, each sized by a macro state vector and filtered independently. The research found one sleeve was losing money structurally rather than randomly, which changed the design more than any amount of tuning would have.
